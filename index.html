ABOUT HAN

Working together for a better community.

Humanity Aid Network (HAN) is a nonprofit, community-focused organization dedicated to humanitarian service and social welfare.

Our activities are built around compassion, volunteerism, transparency and collective action. We aim to connect people who want to help with projects that can make a practical difference.
